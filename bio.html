Junhyun Nam is an architect based in New York. His work moves between building and research: small design-build renovations on one side, a graduate research seminar and lecture series on the other.

He is building a practice that starts with renovations — drawn, permitted, and built by the same hand — and grows toward developing and building his own projects, from a single house outward.

He currently leads a graduate research seminar at SSA–CCNY and is developing a lecture series on the cultures of making, beginning with the production of glass: transparency, culture, meaning, and history.
